PIWCO

Please Invest With Confidence

A mobile game that teaches finance by solving problems: every month a real money problem hits, you decide with real numbers, then a short swipe lesson explains why it worked. Good decisions build your city.

  • The phone on the right is the full app. Click and drag like on a phone (swipe the lesson cards, drag the sliders).
  • The short survey sets your starting point. Rate yourself 1–2 to start with January (budget), 3–5 to go straight to investing and credit.
  • In a hurry? "Skip to game ›" (top right) starts with demo data.
  • Sound is on: turn it off with the speaker icon in the game.
Play it on your phone Scan the code with the camera. It opens full screen in the phone's browser, nothing to install.
